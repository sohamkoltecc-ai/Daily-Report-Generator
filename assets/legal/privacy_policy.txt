PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: August 7, 2026

Daily Report ("Daily Report", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Daily Report desktop application.

Daily Report is designed primarily as a local productivity and record-keeping application. The current version does not require you to create an account or submit your reports to a cloud service operated by us.

By using Daily Report, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.


1. INFORMATION WE HANDLE

Depending on how you use Daily Report, the application may store information that you enter or select, including:

- Your name
- Project names and project information
- Daily report information
- Dates and report entries
- Text entered into reports
- Images or files that you choose to include in reports
- Application preferences and settings
- Other information that you voluntarily enter into the application

You should avoid entering sensitive personal information into reports unless it is necessary for your intended use of the application.


2. HOW YOUR INFORMATION IS STORED

The current version of Daily Report is designed to store your application data locally on your device.

Your projects, reports, settings, and locally referenced files may therefore be stored in files or application storage on your computer.

Daily Report does not currently require an online account to use the core application.


3. NO CLOUD STORAGE BY US

The current version of Daily Report does not provide a cloud storage service operated by us for your reports, projects, images, or other application data.

Data created within the application is primarily stored locally on your device.

If you choose to use another service or application to send, share, back up, synchronize, or otherwise transfer your data, that service may process your information according to its own privacy policy and terms.


4. WE DO NOT SELL YOUR INFORMATION

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We do not use your report content for advertising purposes.

Your locally stored report information is not collected by us merely because you use the application.


5. INTERNET AND ONLINE SERVICES

The current version of Daily Report does not require a cloud account or online server operated by us to store your reports.

If future versions introduce online features, such as cloud synchronization, accounts, analytics, crash reporting, online backup, or other network services, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain what information is collected, how it is used, and how those services operate.


6. IMAGES AND FILES

Daily Report may allow you to select images or other files from your device for use in your reports.

These files are selected by you and are used for the purpose you request, such as displaying or generating a report.

Daily Report does not claim ownership of the images, documents, or other content that you add to the application.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary rights or permissions to use any third-party content you add to your reports.


7. YOUR RESPONSIBILITY FOR YOUR DATA

Because your data may be stored locally on your device, you are responsible for maintaining appropriate backups of important reports and files.

Deleting application data, uninstalling the application, formatting your device, experiencing hardware failure, or otherwise losing access to your storage may result in loss of locally stored information.

We strongly recommend keeping backups of important reports and files.


8. DATA SECURITY

We take reasonable steps in designing Daily Report to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information.

However, no software or computer storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate security on your device, including operating-system security, user accounts, passwords, antivirus protection, and backups where appropriate.


9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Daily Report is a general productivity application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an online service.

Because the current version primarily stores information locally on the user's device, information entered into the application is generally stored on and controlled through that device rather than being transmitted to a cloud service operated by us.
